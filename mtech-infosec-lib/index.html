Malware Analysis
Your pick
Practical Malware Analysis
Sikorski & Honig · No Starch Press · 2012
Not even close — the best book on this list by a margin. Hands-on, assumes competence, teaches you to think like a reverse engineer from page one. x86 disassembly, static/dynamic analysis, anti-RE techniques. Read cover to cover. One of the best infosec books period.

Semester 2 · Elective Your pick
Advanced Penetration Testing & Red Teaming
Your pick (primary — network)
Network Security Assessment, 3rd ed.
Chris McNab · O'Reilly · 2016
Best mental model for network pentest methodology on the list — threat landscape, recon, vuln modelling, network mapping. Structured around how you actually approach an engagement.
Your pick (secondary — web)
The Web Application Hacker's Handbook, 2nd ed.
Stuttard & Pinto · Wiley · 2011
Fills the web pentest depth gap. Covers attack and defence from both sides — still the reference for understanding how web app exploitation actually works at the mechanism level.
Outside rec (fills red team ops gap — critical)
Joe Vest — Red Team Development and Operations · free PDF on GitHub
Covers C2 infrastructure, adversary emulation, rules of engagement — nothing on the syllabus list touches this.
